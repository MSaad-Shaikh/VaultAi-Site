FAQ

A few common questions

VaultAI is designed to process documents with a local Ollama model on the configured computer. Ollama and the model files must be installed first.
The current workflow is designed for PDF, DOCX, and TXT files. Scanned PDFs may need OCR before their text can be searched.
When relevant passages are found, answers can reference the source filename and page where available. You can inspect the supporting passage to check the result.
VaultAI focuses on questions about your own documents. General assistants are still useful for broad knowledge, writing, and web research.
